All-in-One Freight Forwarder Management

Comprehensive logistics management for freight forwarding companies, covering the complete workflow from customer inquiry, quotation, booking, shipment operations, to tracking and profitability analysis.

Key Features

End-to-End Multi-Modal Shipment Management with HBL/MBL control

Advanced Booking & Transport Planning with schedule synchronization

Customs-Compliant Cargo, Container & Dangerous Goods Management

Real-Time Tracking & Customer Self-Service Portal Access

Sales Integration & Revenue Optimization with pricing efficiency

Profitability Reports & Comprehensive Master Data Management

Benefits

Streamline Operations: Automate shipment workflows with unified system and smart reminders. Reduce manual data entry, minimize errors with validation rules, and standardize processes across all forwarding operations.
Enhance Customer Experience: Provide real-time tracking visibility and self-service portal for customers. Proactive status updates keep all stakeholders informed throughout the shipping journey.
Maximize Profitability: Track costs and margins per shipment to identify profitable routes and customers. Handle growing volumes without increasing operational complexity while maintaining service quality.
Ensure Compliance & Integration: Maintain proper documentation for customs, dangerous goods, and regulatory requirements. Seamlessly integrate with Sales, Accounting, and other business modules.

End-to-End Multi-Modal Shipment Management

Complete shipment lifecycle management across Sea, Air, Land, and Multi-modal transport. Automatic numbering, HBL/MBL control, flexible workflow stages, and real-time delivery tracking ensure on-time service.

Advanced Booking & Transport Planning

Multi-leg booking with vessel/flight/truck schedule sync. Cutoff alerts, stakeholder management, and seamless Booking-Route-Shipment data flow throughout operations.

Customs-Compliant Cargo & Dangerous Goods

Three cargo input modes with container, seal, dimensions tracking. Complete HS Code, UN Number, DG Class management for customs compliance. Ultra-fast operations with cargo templates.

Real-Time Tracking & Customer Portal

Intuitive Kanban tracking with sidebar for operations team. Timeline view with standardized status templates. Customer self-service portal for 24/7 shipment inquiry with advanced filters.

Sales Integration & Revenue Optimization

Create logistics quotations and auto-generate shipments from sales orders. Full traceability linking order lines to routes. Supplier pricelist integration with transparent collection/disbursement marking.

Profitability Reports & Master Data

Analytic account integration measuring revenue, cost, profit by customer/route/mode. Pivot/Graph reports with profitability analysis. Complete port, airport, route library with logistics UoM. Automated ETD/ETA reminders and base automation for service quality control.
